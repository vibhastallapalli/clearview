# Roadmap: later, not this weekend

Everything we deliberately parked. For the pitch, pick 4–5: **native app, carrier tracking, stronger scan security, deposits + dispute presets, swappable AI models.**

## Product
- Native phone app alongside the web app
- Better receiving-station hardware
- Stronger reasoning models beyond Gemini (AI calls already sit behind one module)
- Risk-based scanning: only scan new suppliers, high-value orders or suppliers with past issues
- Box / pallet-level scanning with barcodes instead of laying out every package

## Disputes and escrow
- Deposits and claim deposits, so stalling and oversized claims cost something
- Deadlock fallbacks as presets both sides pick upfront (Incoterms-style); must not reward waiting
- Automatic release to the supplier when the inspection window passes with no claim
- Dispute history visible to both sides; optional ratings

## Integrations
- Real carrier tracking to start the inspection clock (simulated in the demo)

## Trust and security
- Stronger scan authenticity / anti-tampering beyond binding captures to order + session
- Verified identity so a fresh wallet can't dodge dispute history
- Proper terms of service to make escrow terms legally binding

## From the original brief
- Visible damage detection
- More sensors (temperature, barcode) beyond one
- More suppliers per demo, languages beyond EN/ES, formats beyond PDF/PNG
